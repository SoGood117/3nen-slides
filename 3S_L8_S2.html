① 並べかえて，正しい文を完成させましょう。

A: That's right. I  /  it  /  liked  /  have since I was a child.
→ A: That's right. I have liked it since I was a child.

② 下線部を変えて，ペアで練習しましょう。

A: My favorite color is pink.
B: Pink symbolizes love, right?
A: That's right. I have liked it since I was a child.
B: I see.
